GET TO KNOW YOUR CHAIRMAN

A Leader Committed to Service

Hon. (Princess) Muhibat Rufai-Adeyemi is a proud daughter of the prestigious Rufai Royal Family of Ojo Town, Lagos State.

A woman of purpose, pedigree and proven performance, she is a respected grassroots leader known for her unwavering dedication to public service and community development.

She holds a Diploma in Banking and Finance from the Institute of Management Technology, Lagos, a Bachelor of Science in Sociology and a Master’s Degree in Public Administration from Lagos State University (LASU).

Today, she continues to champion inclusive development and responsible leadership in her capacity as Chairman.
